How to use this Read the symptom column when something is wrong and you do not yet know why. These are ordered by how often they actually happen.

The three-way diagnosis

When a value is wrong, the shadow table tells you whose problem it is:

ComparisonMeansAction
source ≠ shadowWe are behind. A missed change or a stuck watermarkRe-read and send. Our bug
shadow ≠ ShopifySomebody else wrote it — a person in the admin, or another appAn ownership decision, not a bug. Ask, do not overwrite
source ≠ Shopify, shadow agrees with neitherBoth moved. A genuine conflictApply the ownership rule
Why this table is worth the shadow column Without it, "the price is wrong" is unattributable — you cannot tell your own bug from a colleague editing the admin. With it, you can say which, and the conversation changes from blame to a decision.

Rules that prevent most of the above

ownership per FIELD, not per record ── kills flicker absolute values, never deltas ── kills drift a webhook is a TRIGGER, not a source ── kills stale overwrites watermark = highest updatedAt SEEN ── kills silent gaps one clock per source, the source's own ── kills skew loss tombstones for deletion ── kills zombie products shadow written AFTER a confirmed success ── kills permanent wrong values reconcile VALUES with the denominator ── makes the rest detectable
