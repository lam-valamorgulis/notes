The window

#✓StepStop if
1☐Freeze source edits. Confirm with a named personCannot confirm the freeze
2☐Final delta load. Everything changed since the last full loadThe delta is far larger than expected
3☐Download the bulk results file and count successesFailures above your threshold
4☐Reconcile counts. Publish the denominatorA gross mismatch
5☐Reconcile values — prices and stock, per variantAbove the stop condition
6☐Hand-check ten products, including three bestsellers, against the old systemAny money difference in a bestseller
7☐Enable the ongoing sync. Watch one full cycle end to endThe cycle does not complete cleanly
8☐Unfreeze. ← the irreversible stepAnything above is unresolved
9☐Watch reconciliation for a full 24 hoursDrift appears and does not explain itself
Step 6 is the one people skip It is the only check that catches a price that is wrong but believable — the failure automated comparison passes when both systems agree on the wrong number.

Refuse these, whatever the hour

RefuseBecause
Any code changeUntested code, tired author, live store, moment of maximum change. Roll back or accept
"We'll fix that in the morning" for moneyPrices and stock are right or you stop
Skipping reconciliation because counts looked fineCounts are the check that passes when everything is subtly wrong
Unfreezing before step 7 is provenUnfreeze is irreversible; the load is not

The handover document

products expected ____ / loaded ____ / failed ____ variants expected ____ / loaded ____ / failed ____ prices compared ____ / matching ____ / differing ____ stock compared ____ / matching ____ / differing ____ ten hand-checked products (by name): 1. ______________ 2. ______________ 3. ______________ 4. ______________ 5. ______________ 6. ______________ 7. ______________ 8. ______________ 9. ______________ 10. ____________ stop condition met? NO / YES (if yes, why did we continue?) leftover items — EACH NEEDS A NAME AND A DATE: item ______________________ owner __________ by __________ item ______________________ owner __________ by __________

Every number needs its denominator. "12 differences" means nothing; "12 differences out of 40,120 compared" means something. And every leftover needs a name — "we'll sort those out" with nobody attached means they are still wrong in a month.

The morning after

Send this, with the real numbers Here's the record: 40,120 variants expected, 40,120 loaded, 0 failed. Prices compared on all of them, 12 differences, all long-tail and on the list with owners. Stock compared, no differences. I hand-checked ten products against your old system including your three bestsellers. Reconciliation runs again tonight and I'll send the result. This is what "it worked" sounds like as evidence rather than as a feeling — and it is what protects you if something surfaces in week two.
