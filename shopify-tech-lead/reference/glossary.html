Sync and integration

TermMeansLesson
OwnershipWhich system decides a field. Per field, not per recordA3.2
Absolute write"Stock is 12", never "add 3". Makes replay freeA3.3
IdempotentDoing it twice has the same effect as onceA3.3
TombstoneA record saying "this is gone", so deletion is a presence not an absenceA3.3
WatermarkHow far you have read. The highest updatedAt you actually saw, from the source's clockA3.4
InboxA table whose primary key is the message id. Idempotency as a constraintA2.2
ShadowWhat you last successfully sent. Written after confirmation, never beforeA2.2
Mapping registryIdentity table with your id as the hub. Never pairwiseA4.4
ReconciliationComparing source, shadow and Shopify. The only thing that measures correctnessA3.6
Dead-letterWhere terminal failures go, with resolved_at so it is a queue not a landfillA2.3
Fail open / fail closedOpen on information, closed on a promiseA4.1
BacklogQueued work after an outage. Mostly obsolete — collapse to ids and re-readA3.5
Sellable vs shelf stockTwo different numbers. Confusing them is the classic oversellA3.2

Words this Course avoids

AvoidBecauseSay
"Source of truth"People nod without understanding"Which system decides this field?"
"Two-way sync"Agreed as a phrase, undefined as a specA per-field ownership table
"Real-time"Promises something webhooks do not guarantee"Usually seconds, and definitely correct"
"Best practice"An appeal to authority the client does not recogniseName the specific failure
"It depends on the data"True and uselessName the measurable property
