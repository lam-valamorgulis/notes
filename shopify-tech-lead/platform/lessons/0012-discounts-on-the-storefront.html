Discounts on the storefront

The theme displays discounts. It never decides them. And four fields are deprecated for the same reason.

Your win today. You can fix "the line prices don't add up to the total" in one look, because you know exactly which fields lie and why.

How it works

Derive it: where can a discount be decided?

A price shown on a page could come from the theme's arithmetic or from Shopify. Only one of those can be right, because the theme does not know about every discount type, does not know the customer's eligibility, and is not the thing that takes the money.

So the rule is absolute: the theme displays what Shopify calculated. It never calculates. Every discount bug on a storefront is a violation of that sentence — usually by reading a field that sounds like a total and is not.

The deprecated fields, and the one reason behind all of them

Deprecated on line_itemShopify's stated reasonUse instead
priceDiscounts from automatic discounts and discount codes are not includedfinal_price
line_priceSamefinal_line_price
discountsNot all discount types and details are availablediscount_allocations

The pattern is one thing: those fields include Shopify Scripts discounts and not automatic discounts or discount codes. So they were correct in a world where Scripts were how discounts happened, and they are quietly incomplete now.

That is the whole explanation for the classic bug: the line prices do not add up to the total. The theme is summing line_price, which excludes the code the customer entered, while the total comes from Shopify and includes it. Both numbers are "right"; one of them answers a question nobody asked any more.

The fields that actually work

You wantUse
Price before discount, per unit / per lineoriginal_price · original_line_price
Price after discount, per unit / per linefinal_price · final_line_price
Each discount on a line, with its amountline_level_discount_allocations
Total discount on a lineline_level_total_discount
Discounts on the whole cartcart_level_discount_applications

And the display convention: if a discount applies, show the original with a strikethrough alongside the discounted price. A cart-level discount displays between the subtotal and the total, because the subtotal is after line discounts and the total is after cart discounts.

{% for line_item in cart.items %}
  {% if line_item.original_price > line_item.final_price %}
    <s>{{ line_item.original_price | money }}</s>
  {% endif %}
  {{ line_item.final_price | money }}

  {% if line_item.line_level_discount_allocations.size > 0 %}
    {% for allocation in line_item.line_level_discount_allocations %}
      {{ allocation.discount_application.title }}
      −{{ allocation.amount | money }}
    {% endfor %}
  {% endif %}
{% endfor %}

Verified 2026-08-23: the deprecation of price, line_price and discounts with Shopify's own stated reasons and their replacements — Liquid objects: line_item. The correct display fields, line_level_discount_allocations, line_level_total_discount, cart_level_discount_applications, and the subtotal-versus-total placement — Discounts.

One more trap in the same object

On an order, line_item.title returns the title as it was at the time of the order. But line_item.product.title and line_item.variant.title return the current values. So a renamed or deleted product makes those three disagree — and on an old order receipt, only the first one is historically correct.

Answer before reading on A client reports that their cart page shows £120 of line items but a £108 subtotal, and asks whether the discount is being applied twice. Diagnose it without looking at the code.

The discount is applied once and displayed wrongly. The theme is summing a deprecated field — almost certainly line_price — which excludes discount codes and automatic discounts, while the subtotal comes from Shopify and includes them. So the line numbers are pre-discount and the subtotal is post-discount, and the £12 gap is the discount itself. Nothing is double-counted and nothing is broken in the discount engine. Switch the line display to final_line_price and show original_line_price struck through. This is worth recognising on sight, because the client's own theory sends you looking in the wrong place.

Where it hurts

Costs money — summing deprecated fields Line totals that exclude codes and automatic discounts, next to a total that includes them. Customers see inconsistent numbers on the page where they decide to buy, and some of them stop.
Costs money — calculating a discount in the theme The theme cannot know eligibility, combination rules or every discount type. Any arithmetic it does will eventually disagree with what is charged. Display Shopify's numbers only.
Fails with no error — deprecated but still returning data The deprecated fields still produce plausible money values, so nothing breaks visibly. They are simply incomplete, in one direction, only when certain discount types are present. That is why this survives code review.
Fails with no error — title versus product.title On an order, one is historical and the other is current. A receipt built on the wrong one silently rewrites history after a product is renamed.
A cart discount shown in the wrong place The subtotal is after line discounts; the total is after cart discounts. A cart-level discount placed above the subtotal makes the arithmetic look wrong even when every number is right.
Judgement, not documentation — an invalid code is not an error Applying a wrong discount code does not fail loudly at the cart level; the cart comes back looking normal. You have to inspect the resulting cart to see whether the code was actually applied, rather than assuming a successful response means a successful discount. Treat "did it apply?" as a question you must ask, not something the status code answers.

What you say to a client/PM

When the numbers do not add up on the cart page Nothing's charging twice — the discount is applied once and correctly. The line prices are being read from an older field that doesn't include discount codes, while the total does. So the gap you're seeing is the discount. It's a display fix, and I'd like to do it soon, because customers seeing inconsistent numbers at the cart is the worst place for it. Kills the scary theory first, explains the real cause in one sentence, then argues for urgency on the customer's behalf rather than the code's.
When asked to show a "you saved X%" calculation Happy to show it, and I'll take the numbers from Shopify rather than working them out in the page. If the theme does its own maths, it'll eventually disagree with what's actually charged — usually on a promotion, which is exactly when you'd least want it. Says yes, and defends "no arithmetic in the theme" with the moment it would fail.

Remember this

  • The theme displays discounts, never decides them. Every storefront discount bug breaks this rule.
  • price, line_price and discounts are deprecated — they include Scripts discounts only.
  • Use final_price, final_line_price, discount_allocations and their originals for strikethrough.
  • "Line prices don't add up to the total" is this bug, every time. The gap is the discount.
  • line_level_total_discount per line, cart_level_discount_applications for the cart.
  • A cart discount displays between subtotal and total. Subtotal is after line discounts.
  • On an order, title is historical; product.title is current.

If you remember one thing: if the line prices do not add up to the total, somebody read a deprecated field — the gap is the discount.
