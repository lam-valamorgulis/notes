The Map · Backend Mastery Tree

50 puzzles, one blueprint

Mechanism-level backend literacy: the API surface, the database heart, and everything that keeps them alive in production.

The mission: be able to rebuild from a blank page — not just recognize — every leaf puzzle in the blueprint tree: 7 domains, 20 sub-systems, 50 terminal puzzles. The sheet is drawn to be attacked, not admired: a leaf closes only when you can reconstruct its mechanism cold, and any leaf no project forces gets pruned.

The story of the tree

Two of the seven domains are already yours. A backend service is a program on a runtime (1 · Runtime — the event loop, memory, streams) talking over a network (2 · Network — TCP, TLS, DNS, HTTP) — and both transfer almost whole from the frontend strata you already closed. So they are stamped green in the tree: verify cold, do not re-study.

The attack route runs through the other five. It starts at the surface: 3 · API design — the contract your service shows the world: resources and the frozen verb set, idempotency against a lying network, paging and versioning against growth, then validation, OpenAPI, and the world beyond REST. Beneath every API sits 4 · Data — ★ the heart of the tree: Postgres schema design, B-tree indexes, ACID and isolation, the query planner — plus NoSQL judgment, the access layer (ORMs, N+1, pooling, migrations), and Redis caching.

Then the three domains that separate a working service from one you can ship: 5 · Security (identity, the attack surface, secrets), 6 · Architecture (sync vs async, queues, retries, circuit breakers, stateless scale), and 7 · Operations (logs-metrics-traces, containers, graceful shutdown). Route: 3 → 4 → 5 → 6 → 7 — surface first, heart second, armor last.

Domain 3 — API design in progress

Branch: REST (3 leaves — done) → Contracts (2) → Beyond REST (2).

  • 0001 · Resources & verbs lesson
    Freeze the verbs, let the nouns grow. The safe/idempotent contract table, and why caches and load balancers act on the verb's promise — not your code.
  • 0002 · Idempotency lesson
    Networks fail ambiguously, so make repeating harmless: f(f(x)) = f(x), the set-vs-add lens, and idempotency keys — how POST learns to move money safely.
  • 0003 · Paging & versioning lesson
    Bound every collection from day one: offset drift vs cursor bookmarks. Then evolving a live API: additive changes, tolerant readers, and where the version lives.
  • Still ahead in this domain: validation & error shape, OpenAPI spec, GraphQL vs gRPC, WebSockets (protocol basics).

Domain 4 — Data started

Branch: NoSQL (2 of 4 leaves have a lesson). The Postgres heart, access layer, and caching are still ahead.

  • 0004 · MongoDB: replica sets & sharding lesson
    Copies for survival, slices for capacity: elections by majority, the w:1 rollback trap, retryable writes — then shard keys, the monotonic hot shard, and scatter-gather.

Domain 6 — Architecture started

Branches: Communication (2 of 4 leaves have a lesson) and the new Real-time sub-system (2 leaves).

  • 0005 · Kafka & event-driven architecture lesson
    A log, not a queue: offsets, partitions and the ordering key, the acks=all trap — then events vs commands, and the transactional outbox that fixes the dual-write problem.
  • 0006 · WebSockets at scale lesson
    One pinned connection per user breaks stateless scaling: the pub/sub backplane, fan-out amplification math, heartbeat sweeps, jittered reconnects, and resume-from-sequence.
  • 0007 · WebRTC & real-time media lesson
    Late is worse than lost: UDP media, STUN/TURN/ICE, the mesh-vs-SFU uplink math, and production tricks (replaceTrack, producer-pause mute, simulcast) from a real SFU codebase.

The road ahead

The rest of the territory is on the blueprint. 4 · Data ★ (Postgres schema → indexes → ACID → the planner; document modeling; access layer; Redis) · 5 · Security (sessions vs JWT → OAuth2; injection, XSS/CSRF, rate limiting; argon2, secrets) · 6 · Architecture (sync vs async, queues & pub/sub; retries, idempotency keys, circuit breakers; stateless scale) · 7 · Operations (logs · metrics · traces; containers & CI/CD; graceful shutdown). Domains 1–2 transfer from the frontend strata — quiz-verify only.
