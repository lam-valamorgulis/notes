# Engineering Manager Resources

Curated, high-trust sources. Lessons draw knowledge from here — not from memory.
Communities are where wisdom comes from: real feedback on real situations.

## Knowledge — the core shelf (read these across the year)

- [Book: _The Manager's Path_ — Camille Fournier](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/the-managers-path/9781491973882/)
  The single best starting book for this mission. Walks each stage from mentoring → tech lead →
  managing people → managing managers. Use for: Months 1–6 (the transition, 1:1s, managing people).
- [Book: _The Making of a Manager_ — Julie Zhuo](https://www.juliezhuo.com/book/manager.html)
  Warm, concrete first-90-days view of becoming a manager. Use for: mindset, first 1:1s, running meetings.
- [Book: _An Elegant Puzzle: Systems of Engineering Management_ — Will Larson](https://lethain.com/elegant-puzzle/)
  Systems view: org design, team sizing, sizing work, on-call, migrations. Use for: Months 8–11 (delivery, org design, scaling).
- [Book: _Radical Candor_ — Kim Scott](https://www.radicalcandor.com/the-book/)
  Care personally + challenge directly. Use for: Month 3 (feedback), and any hard conversation.
- [Book: _High Output Management_ — Andrew Grove](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/417697/high-output-management-by-andrew-s-grove/)
  Classic on leverage, output, and 1:1s from Intel's CEO. Use for: Months 5 & 8 (leverage, delivery, metrics thinking).
- [Book: _Accelerate_ — Forsgren, Humble, Kim](https://itrevolution.com/product/accelerate/)
  The research behind the four DORA metrics. Use for: Month 8 (measuring delivery with evidence, not vibes).
- [Book: _Team Topologies_ — Skelton & Pais](https://teamtopologies.com/book)
  Team shapes, cognitive load, and Conway's law. Use for: Month 11 (org design and scaling).
- [Book: _Peopleware_ — DeMarco & Lister](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/peopleware-productive-projects/9780133440706/)
  Timeless on why software problems are people problems. Use for: Month 7 (team health, environment).
- [Book: _97 Things Every Engineering Manager Should Know_ — ed. Camille Fournier (O'Reilly)](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/97-things-every/9781492050896/)
  Short essays incl. "Help Yourself to Better One-on-Ones" (Fournier, Hogan, Irde). Use for: quick, targeted reads per topic.

## Knowledge — blogs & references (free, high-signal)

- [Lara Hogan — Management resources](https://larahogan.me/management/)
  The best free library on 1:1s, feedback, and sponsorship. Start with
  [1:1 resources](https://larahogan.me/resources/one-on-ones/),
  [first-1:1 questions](https://larahogan.me/blog/first-one-on-one-questions/),
  and [feedback resources](https://larahogan.me/resources/feedback/). Use for: Months 2–4.
- [Will Larson — lethain.com](https://lethain.com/) and [StaffEng](https://staffeng.com/)
  Deep, honest essays on management and technical leadership. Use for: delivery, org, and the IC↔manager choice.
- [The Pragmatic Engineer — Gergely Orosz](https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/)
  Higher-signal newsletter on how real tech teams and managers work. Use for: current, grounded practice.
- [Google re:Work — Guides](https://rework.withgoogle.com/en/guides)
  Research-backed guides: Project Aristotle (psychological safety), 1:1s, manager effectiveness. Use for: Month 7.
- [Google DORA — Four Keys / research](https://dora.dev/)
  The authoritative home of the DORA metrics and the yearly State of DevOps report. Use for: Month 8.
- [The Engineering Manager — James Stanier](https://www.theengineeringmanager.com/)
  Practical, first-manager focused articles and the book _Become an Effective Software Engineering Manager_. Use for: Months 1–6.

## Wisdom — communities (test your judgment on real situations)

- [Rands Leadership Slack](https://randsinrepose.com/welcome-to-rands-leadership-slack/)
  ~Thousands of engineering leaders; started by Michael Lopp. Highest-volume peer help. Use for:
  "how would you handle X?" gut-checks, feedback wording, hard-conversation rehearsal.
- [LeadDev](https://leaddev.com/)
  Articles, talks, and events for engineering leaders (StaffPlus, LeadDev conferences). Use for:
  seeing how experienced EMs frame problems; some free, some paid.
- [Plato](https://www.platohq.com/)
  1:1 mentorship marketplace pairing new EMs with senior leaders. Use for: private advice on a specific situation.
- Local / internal: your own manager and a peer EM at work.
  Use for: the single highest-value feedback loop — ask to shadow a 1:1, review your growth plan, or debrief a hard call.
