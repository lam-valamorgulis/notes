A separate course inside the EM course This is its own self-contained course, living in a sub-folder of the Engineering Manager course. The two share one mindset — your impact is now through others — but aim at different roles: that one leads by authority and does people-ops; this one leads by influence and owns front-end technical direction.

The big picture — how it all connects

Everything here grows from one idea: a tech lead's success is no longer their own code but the quality of the team's technical decisions — and you shape those decisions through influence, not authority. Once you accept that, the first job is learning to move a group without power: writing a clear RFC, hearing objections, and getting people aligned. Then you grow the people around you through mentoring and code review that teaches — because a lead who is the only good engineer has no leverage.

On that foundation you take ownership of the front-end itself: its architecture and rendering, its performance, and its design system. Next you raise the whole team's bar — a testing strategy, an accessibility baseline, and predictable delivery. Finally you scale your impact past one codebase: how the front-end splits as it grows, technical strategy and leading risky change, and managing yourself — ending by landing the role. Two themes repeat all year: you lead by influence, not by being the boss, and the move that keeps the team dependent on you is the wrong one.
