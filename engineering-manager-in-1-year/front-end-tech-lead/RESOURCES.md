# Front-End Tech Lead Resources

Curated, high-trust sources. Lessons draw knowledge from here — not from memory.
Communities are where wisdom comes from: real feedback on real technical decisions.

## Knowledge — leadership by influence (the "lead" half of the role)

- [Book: _The Manager's Path_ — Camille Fournier](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/the-managers-path/9781491973882/)
  Has a dedicated **Tech Lead** chapter and treats "mentor → tech lead" as where leadership is
  first learned. Use for: Months 1–3 (the role, mentoring, code-review culture).
- [Book: _The Staff Engineer's Path_ — Tanya Reilly](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/the-staff-engineers-path/9781098118723/)
  The best modern book on leading as a senior IC without authority: scope, influence, writing,
  "big-picture" thinking, and glue work. Use for: Months 2, 11, 12 (influence, strategy, the promo case).
- [StaffEng — Will Larson](https://staffeng.com/) and [lethain.com](https://lethain.com/)
  The archetypes of technical leadership (Tech Lead, Architect, Solver, Right Hand), how to
  operate at Staff+, and engineering strategy. Use for: Months 1, 2, 11.
- [Pat Kua — "Talking with Tech Leads" and blog](https://www.patkua.com/blog/the-definition-of-a-tech-lead/)
  Defined the Tech Lead role and the idea of leading through influence and technical direction.
  Use for: Month 1 (what a Tech Lead actually is).
- [Book: _Radical Candor_ — Kim Scott](https://www.radicalcandor.com/the-book/)
  Care personally + challenge directly. Use for: Month 3 (mentoring, code-review feedback) and any hard conversation.
- [The Pragmatic Engineer — Gergely Orosz](https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/)
  High-signal on how real teams write RFCs/design docs, plan, and run migrations. Use for: Months 2, 9, 11.

## Knowledge — front-end technical depth (the "front-end" half of the role)

- [patterns.dev — Addy Osmani & Lydia Hallie](https://www.patterns.dev/)
  Rendering patterns (CSR/SSR/SSG/ISR/streaming), React patterns, and performance patterns.
  Use for: Month 4 (architecture) and Month 5 (performance).
- [web.dev — Learn Performance & Core Web Vitals (Google)](https://web.dev/explore/learn-core-web-vitals)
  The authoritative home of LCP, INP, and CLS and how to measure and fix them. Use for: Month 5.
- [web.dev — Learn Accessibility (Google)](https://web.dev/learn/accessibility) and
  [W3C WAI / WCAG](https://www.w3.org/WAI/)
  How to build accessible UI and what the WCAG standard actually requires. Use for: Month 8.
- [Brad Frost — _Atomic Design_ (free online)](https://atomicdesign.bradfrost.com/)
  The mental model for design systems: atoms → molecules → organisms → templates → pages.
  Use for: Month 6 (design systems).
- [Design Tokens — W3C Community Group](https://www.designtokens.org/) and
  [Storybook docs](https://storybook.js.org/docs)
  The token standard and the standard tool for building and documenting a component library.
  Use for: Month 6.
- [Kent C. Dodds — "The Testing Trophy" & Testing Library](https://kentcdodds.com/blog/the-testing-trophy-and-testing-classifications)
  and [Playwright docs](https://playwright.dev/)
  A pragmatic front-end testing strategy and the tools to run it. Use for: Month 7.
- [React docs](https://react.dev/) · [Next.js docs](https://nextjs.org/docs)
  Primary-source docs for the assumed default stack. Use for: Months 4–7 (verify APIs, don't guess).
- [Micro Frontends — martinfowler.com (Cam Jackson)](https://martinfowler.com/articles/micro-frontends.html)
  The canonical write-up of the pattern and its real costs. Use for: Month 10.
- [Book: _Team Topologies_ — Skelton & Pais](https://teamtopologies.com/book)
  Team shapes, cognitive load, and Conway's law — how front-end work should be split. Use for: Month 10.
- [Nx](https://nx.dev/) / [Turborepo](https://turbo.build/repo/docs) docs
  The standard tools for large front-end monorepos. Use for: Month 10.
- [Google Engineering Practices — Code Review](https://google.github.io/eng-practices/review/)
  A trusted standard for what a good, teaching code review looks like. Use for: Month 3.

## Wisdom — communities (test your judgment on real decisions)

- [Rands Leadership Slack](https://randsinrepose.com/welcome-to-rands-leadership-slack/)
  Thousands of engineering leaders, including many Staff+ ICs and Tech Leads. Use for: "how would
  you drive this decision / word this RFC / handle this disagreement?" gut-checks.
- [Reactiflux (Discord)](https://www.reactiflux.com/)
  Large, high-quality React/front-end community. Use for: framework and architecture questions.
- [LeadDev / StaffPlus](https://leaddev.com/)
  Articles and talks aimed at senior ICs and technical leaders. Use for: seeing how experienced
  Tech Leads frame problems; some free, some paid.
- Local / internal: an existing Tech Lead or Staff engineer at your company, plus your manager.
  Use for: the highest-value loop — ask to review your RFC, shadow a design review, or debrief a decision.
