# Teaching notes & preferences

Working notes for how to teach Lam. Update as preferences emerge.


## How Lam likes to learn (from the sibling courses in ../)
- **Judgment-first.** Prefers "read a real scenario → pick the best move → know why the
  other options are worse" over abstract theory. Mirror the CCAR-F course style.
- **Beautiful, printable, Tufte-style** lessons. Reuse `assets/base.css` + `assets/quiz.js`;
  never invent per-lesson CSS.
- **Plain English.** Lam is a non-native English reader. Use simple, common words and short
  sentences in docs. Keep real terms (1:1, DORA, PIP, sponsorship) but define each once in
  the glossary and on first use.
- **Practical use case every lesson** — something to try at work this week, even with no reports.

## Global user rules that apply here
- "Never guess — check the source." Cite trusted resources in every lesson (see RESOURCES.md).
- Keep `index.html` (The Map) in sync whenever content is added/renamed/removed — that is part of "done".

## Teaching approach for THIS topic
- Management is **skills-heavy**, not knowledge-heavy. Lean on retrieval drills, scenario
  practice, and real actions — not just reading. Difficulty is the tool for durable skill.
- Because Lam has no reports yet, Months 1–3 include "do it now with no team" versions of each
  skill (mentee, tech-lead a project, glue work) so practice starts immediately.
- Spacing + interleaving: bring back earlier skills (1:1s, feedback) inside later scenario drills.
