# Mission: Become an Engineering Manager within 1 year

## Why
Lam is a strong senior individual-contributor engineer (frontend/backend, TypeScript)
with no direct reports yet. The goal is to be **ready and credible to step into a
front-line Engineering Manager role within 12 months** — able to lead a small team,
grow the people on it, and own delivery — whether that happens by internal promotion
or by moving to a new company. This is not "learn management theory"; it is "be the
obvious choice when a team needs a manager."

## Success looks like
- Run a useful weekly 1:1 and a productive team meeting without a script.
- Give clear, kind, specific feedback — praise and correction — in the moment, not saved up.
- Coach an engineer through a growth goal and delegate real work without becoming a bottleneck.
- Run a structured, fair hiring loop: write a scorecard, interview, and make a defensible decision.
- Explain the team's health and delivery to leadership using evidence (e.g. DORA metrics), not vibes.
- Read a real management scenario and pick the **most effective** move, and say why the other options are worse.
- Have concrete evidence of leadership (led a project, mentored someone, ran an incident) to point to in an interview or promo case.

## Constraints
- **Time:** 6–10 hours per week, alongside a full-time engineering job.
- **Starting point:** senior IC, no formal reports; leads informally at best.
- **Target:** broad EM foundations that work in either a startup/scale-up or a larger company (specialise later).
- **Format:** matches the other courses in `../` — an `index.html` "Map" as the single
  source of truth, HTML lessons, quick-reference docs, and learning records. Judgment-first,
  scenario-driven (read a situation → pick the best move → know why the rest are worse).
- **Practical over theoretical:** every lesson must include a real use case to act on this week.

## Out of scope (for now — protects focus)
- Managing managers / director-and-above skills (that is the *next* year, after front-line EM).
- Deep finance, legal, or HR-compliance detail specific to one country or company.
- Founder / CEO / general-business strategy beyond what an EM needs to run one team.
- Becoming a Staff+ IC instead — this workspace assumes the management track is the goal.
