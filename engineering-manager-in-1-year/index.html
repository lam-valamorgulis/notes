The big picture — how it all connects

Everything here grows from one idea: a manager's success is no longer their own output but the team's output. Once you accept that shift, the job splits into four hats — People, Delivery, Direction, and Process — that you wear all day. Knowing the hats also warns you where new managers fail: the four transition traps of doing too much or too little. And because skill comes from reps, not titles, you start practicing now — mentoring, leading a project, running a retro — before you ever have a report.

From that foundation the year walks through the hats in order. First the People hat, because engineers neglect it most: the weekly 1:1, then feedback, then coaching and growth, performance, and hiring. Then we zoom out to the team as a system — its health and safety, its delivery, and your technical leadership. Finally you scale your impact outward: managing up, org design, and managing yourself — ending by landing the role. Two themes repeat all year: the human hats matter most, and the move that feels good right now often weakens the team.
