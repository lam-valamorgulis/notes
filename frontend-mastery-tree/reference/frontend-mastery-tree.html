Cores you can derive from cold memory. Tap a core to lock it in. No credit for recognition — only for reconstruction.

    Reading the tree. Branch nodes are territory. Leaf nodes in mono are the terminal cores — each one is small enough to explain on a whiteboard in five minutes with no notes, and load-bearing enough that missing it corrupts every layer above.

    The gate rule. A core is locked in only when you can (1) state the mechanism, (2) predict an output before running it, and (3) point at it in a real profile, trace, or spec. Two out of three is zero out of three.

    Traversal order. Depth-first, left to right. Strata 1→3 are physics; 4 is architecture built on that physics; 5→8 are the constraints that separate a working app from a shippable one. Marks are saved in this browser (localStorage), so your scorecard survives a reload.
