Lesson 16 · Day 3 · The Mock

Full Mock Interview

Four rounds, the whole loop. Answer out loud, reveal the model answer, then grade yourself the way the interviewer will.

How to run this mock: treat it like the real room. Read each question, then answer it out loud — full sentences, no notes — as if the interviewer is watching your face. Only after you have spoken, tap the card to reveal a model answer and compare: did you hit the same points, in a clear order? Do all four rounds in one sitting to build stamina. Round 3 is multiple-choice — answer from memory, then read why.
Scoring rubric — how a senior answer scores Grade yourself on the four things a senior answer always has: (1) Structure — you lead with a one-line answer, then expand in a logical order (not a random pile of facts). (2) Correct facts — exact names and numbers (1000-point bucket, 50/sec restore, HMAC-SHA256, X-Shopify-Webhook-Id), not hand-waving. (3) Trade-offs — you say why you chose X over Y and what it costs. (4) Failure modes — you name what breaks (throttling, lost webhooks, ERP down) and how you handle it. Miss one and it reads as mid-level. Hit all four and it reads as senior.
The gate — answer BEFORE you reveal Do not tap a card open until you have said your full answer out loud. If you go blank, give yourself a 20-second think-timer, then speak — even a rough answer. Reading the model first feels good and teaches you nothing. Honour this on every card.
Score sheet — tick after you speak, then print or copy it Strong pass = 3 or more of the 4 ticked on every question, said ALOUD before revealing. One tick each for structure, correct facts, trade-offs, failure modes.
