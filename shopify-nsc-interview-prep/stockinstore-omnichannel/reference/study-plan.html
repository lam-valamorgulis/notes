stockinstore Sub-Course · Standalone Study Plan

3 Days Through the stockinstore Domain

All 11 lessons, 4 katas, and 4 reference cards are already built. This plan is the order to move through them so ideas stick — concept, then code, then recall — ending with a full design mock.

The plan in one line. Day 1 builds the data foundation (the domain, getting stock in, answering the shopper). Day 2 builds the transaction and reliability layers (buying, fulfilment, scale). Day 3 turns all of it into one whiteboard answer and drills it under a mock.
Why the order differs slightly from the Map index.html groups all four katas under Chapter 6 ("The Interview") because that is where they sit in the site's table of contents. This plan instead does each kata right after its matching lesson (K1 after L2, K2 after L4, K3 after L5, K4 after L6) — coding the idea while it is fresh beats coding four ideas back-to-back at the end.

learn read the lesson   code build the matching kata from a blank file   drill recall out loud / mock

Day 1 — The domain & getting stock in~3.5–4 hours

Goal: know why store stock is hard to trust, how it gets ingested cleanly, and how a shopper's "in stock near me?" gets answered fast.

learnL1 · The omnichannel problem & domain — four products, six actors, the one hard truth. (20m)
learnL11 · Integrating Shopify with the stockinstore SaaS — Track B, in case the client buys rather than builds. (20m)
learnL2 · Inventory ingestion & multi-source sync — feeds → canonical model → idempotent upsert. (25m)
codeK1 · Idempotent inventory upsert, typed from blank, narrating each guard out loud. (45m)
learnL3 · The stock availability engine — on-hand vs ATP, thresholds, "call to confirm". (25m)
learnL4 · Find in Store — the read path — geospatial query + pre-computed availability + cache. (25m)
codeK2 · Find-in-store query API, typed from blank. (45m)
drillRecall check: say the ingestion pipeline and the ATP formula out loud, no notes. (15m)

Day 2 — Buying, fulfilment & reliability at scale~4–4.5 hours

Goal: know how a browse becomes a kept promise (reservation, pickup, allocation) and what keeps all of it correct under load and failure.

learnL5 · Click & Collect / BOPIS order flow — reserve, PICK_UP order, pick, notify. (25m)
codeK3 · Click & Collect reservation, atomic all-or-nothing, typed from blank. (45m)
learnL6 · Ship from Store & the OMS fairness allocation — score, prefer one shipment, reassign. (25m)
codeK4 · Order-allocation fairness scorer, typed from blank. (45m)
learnL7 · Event-driven backbone — queues, work-queue vs pub/sub, the four reliability rules. (25m)
learnL8 · Data model & consistency — schema, source-of-truth per fact, the outbox. (30m)
learnL9 · Scale, multi-tenancy & failure modes — three workloads, tenant isolation, SLOs. (30m)
drillRecall check: say the four reliability rules and one row of the failure table out loud. (15m)

Day 3 — The interview: one system, one story~3 hours

Goal: turn nine lessons of detail into one confident whiteboard answer, then prove it holds up under a mock.

learnL10 · "Design stockinstore" — the 7-step walkthrough and the 8-sentence summary. (40m)
drillDesign cheat sheet — say the 7 steps and 3 paths out loud, cover-and-recall. (20m)
drillData model card — recall the schema and the consistency split from memory. (15m)
drillGlossary — cover-and-recall every acronym (SKU, POS, ERP, ATP, OMS…). (15m)
drillRapid-fire Q&A — full pass, ~22 questions, answer before reveal. (35m)
drillSay "mock me on stockinstore" — a full "design stockinstore" whiteboard mock with "why?" follow-ups. (45m)
learnFix pass: re-read whichever lesson the mock exposed as thin. (20m)
