The 7-Day Client-Round Plan

Several candidates. One hire. This week is not about knowing more facts — it is about being the person the client trusts to own their Shopify work. That is what gets you picked.

The plan in one line. Your technical is already strong, so keep it warm with a short daily review + coding rep — refresh the concept, then type it. Do not over-drill it. Spend the real hours on the three things that win: confidence under pressure, owning the delivery, and standing out. End with two full mocks.
How the week is weighted (your ranking)
HIGH — confidence · management · standout · mocks (~36h) MED — behavioral depth (~9h) LOW — technical, review + kept warm (~11h)

learn read / new depth   drill say it out loud / role-play / mock   tech technical review + keep-warm coding rep

The identity you are building all week Every day points at one person: the senior who owns outcomes, explains hard things simply, has deep Shopify scars, and never needs chasing. Facts are table stakes — this identity is the differentiator. Say it out loud until it feels true.
Where the technical review lives Technical is LOW priority but never skipped. Each day has one tech block that reviews one topic, then codes it. Across Days 1–5 this refreshes every area the client can probe: Shopify APIs, webhooks, rate limits, auth, the ERP-sync design, databases, API design, messaging, DevOps, and testing. The two mocks then test it under pressure.

Day 1 — Confidence & thinking out loud8 hours · HIGH

Goal: build the one skill that wins live rounds — staying calm and talking clearly when you do not instantly know the answer.

learnStudy, at depth: L29 · Confident problem-solving under pressure. Learn the narration habit, the time-buying phrases, the recovery ladder, and the senior "I don't know". (3h)
drillRehearse the four hard moments out loud until automatic: buy time · climb the recovery ladder · a senior "I don't know" · recover from a bug you wrote. Record yourself once; check pace and pauses. (2h)
techReview: Shopify APIs, webhooks & rate limits — take the Day-1 diagnostic and skim the Shopify cheat sheet, saying each "why" out loud. This is your technical baseline — every miss is a topic to review later in the week. (1.5h)
techCode: Kata 1 · Webhook handler, typed from blank out loud, plan first. Not just for the code — for the narration. (1.5h)

Day 2 — Own the delivery I: client & scope8 hours · HIGH

Goal: sound like the senior who takes a vague request and turns it into safe, visible delivery — the client's biggest worry, solved.

learnDepth pass: L19 · Owning ambiguous scope + L22 · Communicating with a busy client. (3h)
drillRole-play scenarios 1, 2 & 4 from the Management Scenarios Drill — vague scope, scope creep, silent client. Say each answer out loud; land "make it visible · name the risk · options + a recommendation". (2.5h)
techReview + code: re-read (skim) L3 · App auth + L5 · Integration patterns, say the "why", then code Kata 4 · NestJS orders module. (2h)
drillDraft one STAR story about owning a messy, unclear project. Rough is fine — you polish it on Day 4. (0.5h)

Day 3 — Own the delivery II: time & risk8 hours · HIGH

Goal: show you can run the work like a lead — estimate honestly, protect focus, and flag risk before it bites.

learnDepth pass: L20 · Estimating & pacing solo + L21 · Multi-project focus. (3h)
drillRole-play scenarios 3, 5 & 6 from the Management Scenarios Drill — a missed estimate, competing projects, a risky backfill. Practise flagging bad news early and de-risking a big change. (2h)
techReview the set piece: redraw L11 · Shopify ↔ ERP sync from memory + skim the system-design cheat sheet. This is the design the client digs into hardest. Then code Kata 3 · Idempotent sync worker. (2h)
drillSay "mock me — how I run my work". I ask the "how do you estimate / handle a slip / juggle projects" questions and grade you. (1h)

Day 4 — Behavioral depth8 hours · MEDIUM

Goal: senior-level stories that carry a trade-off and a "what I'd do differently" — not just "I did a task".

learnRe-read L15 · Senior narrative; use the STAR worksheet to rebuild your 3 best stories to senior depth. (3.5h)
drillAdd two more stories from L17 · Teamwork + L28 · Mentoring & code review — a conflict story and a mentoring story. Say each out loud, timed to ~2 minutes. (2h)
techReview + code: recall L9 · Databases + L8 · API design (say when you'd pick SQL vs NoSQL, and 3 REST design rules), then code Kata 2 · Debug a race condition. (2h)
drillPrepare your questions to ask them — sharp, senior questions that show you are already thinking about their business. (0.5h)

Day 5 — Stand out: your four differentiators8 hours · HIGH

Goal: turn "owner mindset · clear communication · deep Shopify · proactive & reliable" from words into concrete moves and phrases you actually use in the room.

learnStudy L26 · Be the one they pick. For each of your four differentiators, write two concrete moves and one line you will say. (3h)
drillPolish your Shopify war stories — rate limits, duplicate webhooks, the backfill that hammered the API. Say each as a crisp soundbite that proves "I've shipped this in production". (2h)
techBreadth review + code: rapid refresh of L12 · Messaging + L13 · DevOps + L14 · Testing using the rapid-fire drills, then code Kata 5 · Retry & dead-letter. (2h)
drillRecall check: say your four differentiators, your 3 STAR stories, and your questions-to-ask from memory. (1h)

Day 6 — Full Mock #1: the mixed round8 hours · HIGH

Goal: rehearse the real thing once, cold, and find what is still shaky while there is time to fix it.

drillSay "mock me — mixed". I run a full mock: a live-coding task + a system-design dig (the technical Q&A under pressure) + a management scenario + behavioral. See L16 for the format. (3h)
drillGrade & debrief: where did composure slip, where did you go silent, where was a story flat, which technical answer was thin? Write the fixes. (2h)
techFix + re-code: re-read any technical topic the mock exposed as weak, then re-code, from blank, any exercise you stumbled on — out loud. (2h)
drillReset: re-say your weakest three answers until they are smooth. Then stop and rest. (1h)

Day 7 — Full Mock #2: client-style, then polish8 hours · HIGH

Goal: make the real rounds feel like a repeat. Rehearse the whole arc — open, work, close — so you walk in calm.

drillSay "mock me — client style". Full client-style mock, harder follow-ups, "why?" three times deep on the technical answers. I grade you and list fixes. (3h)
drillFix the weak spots the mock found. Redo any answer or exercise that wobbled. (2h)
drillStandout rehearsal: your opening ("here's who I am"), your four differentiators woven in, your questions to ask, and a strong close. Run the whole arc twice. (2h)
learnRead the Night-Before One-Pager. Say your soundbites. Sleep. You are ready. (1h)
If time gets tight Never-skip core: the confidence / think-out-loud drill (Day 1) · the management scenarios · your 4 differentiators + war stories · the two mocks. These carry the client rounds. The tech blocks can shrink to ~1 hour (review only, skip the kata) if a day runs over — but never drop them to zero, or the technical goes cold and the "why?" questions catch you out.
I'm your teacher and your mock interviewer. Say "start day 1" to begin, "next" to move on, "drill me under pressure" or "role-play a client scenario" any time, "go deeper on <topic>" to review a technical area, and "mock me" when you want the full run. Paste anything new the client tells you and I re-target the week.
