The 3-Day Schedule

10 hours a day × 3 days = 30 hours. Built the way memory actually works: short lessons, then hard recall, spacing, interleaving, and a full mock. Interview target ~2026-07-12.

The science baked into this schedule. You don't learn by reading more — you learn by struggling to recall. So every learning block is followed by a practice block where you close the tab and say the answer out loud. Each day starts by recalling yesterday (spacing). Day 3 mixes all topics (interleaving). This beats 30 hours of re-reading, by a lot.

learn new material   drill recall / practice   rest break — take it, your brain consolidates during breaks
