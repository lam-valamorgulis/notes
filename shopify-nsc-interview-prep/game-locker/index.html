Game Locker — The Map

A 24-hour interview sprint for one specific job: Shopify Engineer at Game Locker. Seven lessons and five reference pages, aimed only at what this company needs — and routed through the 52 lessons already in the parent course.

Start here: The 24-Hour Plan — hour-by-hour, with what to read and what to say out loud at the end of each block. Everything else on this page is reached from there.

The story this sub-course tells

Game Locker is not a Shopify store. It is the platform game stores run their business on — 145 million cards, daily repricing, a POS kiosk at the counter — and Shopify is a channel hanging off it. So this role is three arrows: keep the two systems in agreement, stop a card selling twice, and build the storefronts collectors actually use. Plus a marketplace they are building now, which Shopify does not natively support.

What this sub-course deliberately does not do

  • It does not re-teach the parent course. Webhooks, app auth, bulk operations, NestJS, testing, system design — those are already written. This sub-course points at them.
  • It does not re-teach Liquid syntax. theme-architecture covers what changed recently and what is easy to get wrong; L05 applies it to many-merchants-one-theme and to performance, which was the real gap.
  • It does not claim POS app experience. The honesty line — read one closely, found real bugs, never authored one — is used consistently, exactly as in POS L05 and POS L08.
  • It does not invent facts about Game Locker. Everything in the company brief is from their public site or the job description. Inferences are labelled as inferences.
