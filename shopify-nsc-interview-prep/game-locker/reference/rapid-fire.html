Reference · Drill · Block 21

Rapid Fire — 50 Questions

Twenty seconds each, spoken out loud, tap to check. Target 40 out of 50. Mark the misses and re-run only those.

How to use this: read the question, say the answer out loud before tapping. Reading the answer without speaking first trains recognition, not recall — and the interview needs recall.

Grouped: 1–10 Game Locker & domain · 11–20 inventory & POS · 21–28 sync mechanics · 29–36 APIs & webhooks · 37–44 theming & performance · 45–50 marketplace.
