The 7-Day Plan

Seven days, about eight hours each. Every block has one job and one thing you must be able to say out loud at the end of it. Your ticks are saved in this browser.

The whole week in one line: three days on the React the job description asks for, one day turning your real-time project into your strongest answer, one day on the surrounding craft, one day on working with people, and one day rehearsing it all out loud.

Progress: 0 / 0 blocks done

The rule that makes this work Half of every day is spoken, not read. Reading a lesson and thinking "I know that" is exactly the illusion this week exists to break. If a block says out loud, do it out loud, alone in a room, at full volume. It feels ridiculous. It is the only thing that transfers to the interview.
